Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

In short: Ceppty keeps your receipts on your phone. No account, no ads, no tracking. Data only leaves your phone when you choose to send it: a report about a receipt the app can't read, or something you share yourself.

Who we are

Ceppty is made by ZixyAero (KvK 97721379, the Netherlands). Privacy questions: support@ceppty.nl.

What data the app uses

  • Receipts you add. The app reads the text from the PDF or photo (store, date, products, prices, discounts) and stores it in a database on your phone.
  • Your settings, such as your own categories and the category you give a product.

The app does not keep the PDF or photo itself: the copy it makes is deleted once the receipt is read and confirmed. Your original file stays where it was. Exception: for a receipt the app couldn't read correctly, it keeps the file on your phone until you send a report or delete the receipt, at most 30 days.

What we don't do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • Nothing is sent in the background — the app only connects to the internet when you tap “Send report”.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting, no ads, no tracking.
  • No selling or passing data to third parties.

Reports about a receipt

If the app reads a receipt incorrectly, you can send us a report. Before sending you choose what it contains: the receipt text, technical details about how the app read it, and — only if you tick it — the PDF or photo itself. A photo shows everything printed on the receipt. The app removes loyalty card and payment card numbers from the text before sending. You can add a name so we know who you are; there is no account.

The report goes to our own server in the EU. We use it only to fix how the app reads receipts, keep it for at most 90 days and then delete it. To have your report deleted sooner, email us the report number the app shows after sending. We don't sell or share reports with anyone.

When you share something yourself

You can also send files through your phone's share menu, for example an export of your spending. This only happens when you choose to, and you choose who receives it.

Deleting your data

You can delete receipts in the app. Uninstalling the app erases all of its data from your phone. We don't have a copy.

Children

The app is not meant for children and asks nobody for personal details.

Changes

If anything in this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.

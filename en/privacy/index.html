Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

In short: Ceppty keeps everything on your phone only. We collect nothing: no account, no ads, no tracking. Data only leaves your phone when you share it yourself.

Who we are

Ceppty is made by ZixyAero (KvK 97721379, the Netherlands). Privacy questions: support@ceppty.nl.

What data the app uses

  • Receipts you add. The app reads the text from the PDF (store, date, products, prices, discounts) and stores it in a database on your phone.
  • Your settings, such as your own categories and the category you give a product.

The app does not keep the PDF file itself: the copy it makes while importing is deleted right afterwards. Your original file stays where it was.

What we don't do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No data sent to a server — the app makes no internet connection with your data.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting, no ads, no tracking.
  • No selling or passing data to third parties.

When you share something yourself

Some features create a file you can send through your phone's share menu, for example an export of your spending or a report about a receipt the app can't read correctly. This only happens when you choose to, and you choose who receives it. If you send such a report to us, we use it only to improve the app and delete it once that's done. You can always ask us to delete your message sooner.

Deleting your data

You can delete receipts in the app. Uninstalling the app erases all of its data from your phone. We don't have a copy.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If anything in this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.

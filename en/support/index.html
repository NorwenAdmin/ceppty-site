Support

Question, problem or idea? Email support@ceppty.nl. We usually reply within a few working days.

Adding a receipt

  1. Open the Albert Heijn app and go to your receipts.
  2. Open a receipt and tap Share.
  3. Choose Ceppty — the receipt is added right away.

Already saved the PDF? In Ceppty, tap Import files and choose one or more PDFs.

FAQ

Which stores are supported?
For now, only Albert Heijn digital receipts (PDF).

A receipt isn't read correctly.
Send us a report from the app or email us. Remove anything you don't want to share first.

Where is my data?
Only on your phone. See the privacy policy.

Does the app cost anything?
No. Ceppty is free and has no ads.
